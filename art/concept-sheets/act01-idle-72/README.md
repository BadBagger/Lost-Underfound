# Act 1 72-Frame Idle Concept Sheets

Standalone generated concept grids for Act 1 idle animation review.

These are not runtime-sliced production sheets yet. They are visual direction
targets for real pose-to-pose animation: breathing, weight shifts, blinks,
secondary motion, and character-specific idle business on flat hot pink
backgrounds for easy isolation.

Included characters:

- `pip-idle-72-grid.png`
- `bramble-idle-72-grid.png`
- `old-bottlecap-idle-72-grid.png`
- `scuttle-idle-72-grid.png`

Grommet is intentionally excluded because Act 1 design/script detail does not
include Grommet as a playable Act 1 character.
